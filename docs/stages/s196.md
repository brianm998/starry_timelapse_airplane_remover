# s196: the new app icon in both clients (ticket #201)

Summary: `art/export.py` now writes the mac AppIcon PNGs and the desktop `star.icns`, `star.ico` and `star.png` from BR-2 (16 to 64 px) and BR-1 (128 px and up), with the body at 824/1024 of the canvas.

- `.icns` and `.ico` are written in pure Python (PNG payloads), so `--check` is deterministic and runs without iconutil/magick; both were verified locally with them.
- BR-2's stray specks are cleared (alpha < 128 outside the body's bounding box) before placement.
- `star.png` is BR-1 at 512 px with the body at 94%.
- No strings change.
