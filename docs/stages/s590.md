# s590: App Store build bundles the LGPL ffmpeg

Summary: `gui/appstore_release.sh` refuses a GPL ffmpeg/ffprobe pair (source pair checked up front, bundled copies grepped for `--enable-gpl`), swaps in the LGPL pair from `APPSTORE_FFMPEG_DIR` with its licence notice, and the `macos-testflight` job now depends on a new `ffmpeg-lgpl` job and its artifact.
